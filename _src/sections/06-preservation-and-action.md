### Protecting Your Media Collection

When media exists only on outside servers, entire eras of film, television, and gaming history are at risk of being lost forever if a platform shuts down (a good example is the recent Ao3 shutdow. Even tho it wasn't permanent, it caused an uproar online).
 Physical discs, cartridges, etc. ensure cultural works remain playable decades later.

Beginners can protect their collections by backing up their personal media, as well as other ways. If you're just starting out and aren't sure what gear or storage habits actually matter, <a href="https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/electronics/everything-you-need-collecting-physical-media">this CNN Underscored beginner's guide to collecting physical media</a> walks through the basic players, sleeves, and care tips you need so your discs and tapes don't get scratched or ruined over time. Just like anything, though, this is just a guide not a set of rules. You can customize as you need or like.
