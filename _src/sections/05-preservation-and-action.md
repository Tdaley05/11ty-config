### Protecting Your Media Collection

When media exists only on outside servers, entire eras of film, television, and gaming history are at risk of being lost forever if a platform shuts down.
 Physical discs, cartridges, etc. ensure cultural works remain playable decades later.

Beginners can protect their collections by backing up their personal media, as well as other ways.
