### Buying vs. Renting Your Media

When you buy a physical music, movies, or video games cartridge, you own a copy outright. You can lend it to a friend, play it offline forever (my favorite part), and keep it on your shelf regardless (since streaming services constantly add and remove media).

When you buy a digital movie or game, you are usually only purchasing the license to access that file, which means the company can alter or remove it at any time.
