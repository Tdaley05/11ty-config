### Buying vs. Renting Your Media

When you buy a physical piece of media like music, movies, or video games, you own a copy outright. You can lend it to a friend, play it offline forever (my favorite part), and keep it on your shelf (since streaming services constantly add and remove media).

When you buy a digital movie or game, you are usually only purchasing the license to access that file, which means the company can alter or remove it at any time. I'm not the only one who doesn't like that, <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/media/2026/oct/02/physical-media-fans-streaming-algorithms-cds-dvds-vinyl">this Guardian piece</a> is full of interviews with everyday CD, tape, and DVD collectors and a great read. Especially the stories about whole shows vanishing off streaming and people ditching Spotify just to stop companies from tracking what they listen to.
