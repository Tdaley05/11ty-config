### The Push Away From Discs and Cartridges

Major console makers and retailers are actively phasing out physical media to cut manufacturing costs and push consumers toward subscriptions and digital games. 
Many new gaming consoles ship without disc drives (such as the next playstation), and big-box stores have dramatically shrunk their physical movie aisles. Second hand stores try to combat this lack of physical media on shelves.

As physical runs get smaller, copies become harder to find at retail price, driving up secondhand market costs and making physical ownership feel like a luxury.
